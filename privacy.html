Visitor privacy

This website uses optional visit statistics to understand its readership.

What is recorded

When visit statistics are enabled, a page view may record your public IP address, approximate country or region and city, the page path, and the visit time. URL query strings and fragments are not recorded.

IP location is obtained through ipapi; the request exposes your public IP address to that service. Visit records are stored in Google Firebase and are accessible to the website administrator. They are not published on this website.

An IP address does not identify a specific person or prove an institutional affiliation. Shared networks, mobile connections, VPNs, blockers and failed requests affect these statistics.

Your choice

Global Privacy Control and Do Not Track signals are respected. You can also disable optional visit statistics in this browser below. This choice is saved on this browser and device; clearing browser storage removes it.

For questions about existing visit records, contact lssongbo.wu@polyu.edu.hk.
