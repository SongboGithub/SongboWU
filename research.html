Reliable deformation measurements from InSAR

I develop methods that improve deformation retrieval, measurement positioning and uncertainty assessment in complex environments.

Published results

Research contributions

  • Developed estimators for coherent-point selection, phase recovery and sequential processing of multi-temporal InSAR observations.
  • Advanced phase unwrapping, atmospheric delay mitigation and multi-platform data fusion for regional and urban monitoring.
  • Applied these methods to deformation investigations in Hong Kong, Zhuhai, Nantong, Beijing, Fengfeng and the Xiluodu Hydropower Station.

My role. Algorithm development and data analysis during my research assistant and PhD work.

02 / 06

Buildings, bridges and transport infrastructure

My work combines spaceborne and ground-based radar to investigate structural deformation and the effects of urban construction.

Published results

Research contributions

  • Identified multi-dimensional deformation anomalies associated with construction at To Kwa Wan station in Hong Kong.
  • Developed a fusion model combining ground-based interferometric radar and inertial vision measurements to retrieve vertical, lateral and longitudinal bridge deformation.
  • Developed a 3D model for jointly correcting repositioning and tilting errors in GPRI-II radar measurements.

Selected outcome. Bridge experiments reported a 27% improvement in accuracy over individual sensor measurements.

My role. Method development, multi-sensor integration and research supervision.

03 / 06

Reclaimed land and coastal consolidation

I use long-term radar observations to investigate uneven settlement and consolidation of reclaimed coastal land.

Published results

Research contributions

  • Combined multiple SAR datasets to reconstruct the long-term settlement of Hong Kong International Airport.
  • Investigated the new third-runway reclamation with PS/DS-InSAR, independent component analysis and Terzaghi consolidation theory.
  • Separated shallow compaction, settlement in marine deposits and thermal effects, and compared Deep Cement Mixing with Prefabricated Vertical Drains.

My role. Principal investigator of the aging infrastructure project; collaborator on the third-runway consolidation study, contributing conceptualization, resources and research supervision.

04 / 06

Regional ground deformation and monitoring suitability

My research maps regional ground instability and assesses where InSAR can provide reliable measurements, with applications to coastal regions, mining areas and the Greater Bay Area.

Published results

Research contributions

  • Developed an improved MT-InSAR framework combining independent component analysis and non-stationary analysis to separate atmospheric effects from deformation signals.
  • Mapped ground deformation in Nantong, in the northern Yangtze River estuary, from 2015 to 2023, examining spatial patterns associated with groundwater extraction, urbanization and reclamation.
  • Extended regional monitoring to densely vegetated mining areas and assessed InSAR applicability across the Greater Bay Area using optical imagery and multiple suitability indices.

Selected outcome. The Nantong study recovered regional deformation with RMSE below 5.6 mm against leveling observations.

My role. Corresponding author of the Nantong regional deformation study.

Learning methods for radar data interpretation

I investigate learning-based approaches to reduce processing limitations and interpret deformation in complex radar datasets.

Published results

Research contributions

  • Investigated automated deformation detection and interpretation using a multi-task vision transformer.
  • Contributed to lightweight 3D CNN and adversarial joint-learning methods for time-series retrieval and phase reconstruction.

My role. Method development and supervision of research on deep learning for InSAR.

06 / 06

From radar algorithms to monitoring platforms

I bring radar analysis together with complementary surveying technologies to support practical land-subsidence monitoring.

Published results

Research contributions

  • Led technical strategy and project management for the integration of InSAR and mobile surveying technologies.
  • Integrated newly developed and existing algorithms into an online web-based service system.
  • Led the design of a vehicle-based ground deformation monitoring platform.
  • Supervised HotSAM, a hotspot-adaptive multi-resolution framework for efficient long-term terrestrial radar monitoring.

Selected outcome. The project received the Hong Kong Smart Living Partnership Award (2023/24) in Smart Building / Environment Technology.

My role. Team leader of the Hong Kong ITC-funded integrated InSAR and mobile surveying project.

Processing efficiency. In the reported HotSAM experiments, processing time fell by 73.04% and storage by 96.95% compared with conventional processing.

Current research

My current research spans sequential InSAR processing, ground stability, tidal-flat topographic change and infrastructure health monitoring.

Sequential InSAR processing

NSFC-funded work on phase closure, sequential phase unwrapping and efficient updates of deformation time series.

MTR ground stability

A satellite monitoring system for Hong Kong MTR, with research into measurement coverage, 3D deformation and timely updates.

Tidal-flat topographic change

Monitoring topographic changes across intertidal flats to quantify how their morphology evolves over time.

Infrastructure health monitoring

Integrating InSAR observations with finite element models to investigate structural behavior and assess infrastructure health.
